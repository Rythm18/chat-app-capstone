# NEET 2026 Exam Paper Leak - Arrests and Investigation Response

## Executive Summary

The NEET-UG 2026 examination, held on May 3, 2026, for over 2.27 million aspirants across 551 Indian cities and 14 overseas centers, was cancelled on May 12, 2026, following large-scale paper leak allegations and security breaches. The Central Bureau of Investigation took over the case and has arrested 13 individuals across 7 states as of June 2026.

## Investigation Agencies and Actions

### Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)

**Timeline:**
- **May 7, 2026**: CBI registered FIR following whistleblower tip-off
- **May 12, 2026**: CBI officially took over investigation from Rajasthan Police Special Operations Group
- **May 13, 2026**: First wave of CBI arrests (5 key accused from Biwal family and associates)
- **May 18, 2026**: CBI arrested Shivaraj Motegaonkar (Latur coaching owner)
- **May 27, 2026**: CBI arrests raised to 13 with arrest of Latur doctor and Pune coaching faculty

**Investigation Scope:**
- Conducted searches at **49 locations** across multiple states
- Seized laptops, mobile phones, and incriminating documents
- Engaged in detailed analysis of seized materials
- Deployed digital forensics teams
- Investigated money trail showing Rs 10 lakh transaction for paper acquisition and Rs 12 lakh for distribution
- Used Air Force aircraft for secure transportation of test papers for June 21 re-exam

### Rajasthan Police Special Operations Group
- Detained **15 people** during investigation
- Conducted initial questioning and raids
- Arrested 5 persons on May 7 and 4 aspirants on May 8
- Investigated in Sikar and nearby areas
- Hundreds of people questioned during investigation

## NTA and Ministry of Education Responses

### National Testing Agency (NTA) Actions

**Official Decisions:**
- **May 12, 2026**: Announced cancellation of NEET-UG 2026 examination
- **Scheduled re-examination**: June 21, 2026
- **Exam scope**: Re-exam for approximately **22.8 lakh (2.28 million) candidates** who appeared on May 3
- **Test centers**: 551 Indian cities and 14 overseas centers

**Administrative Actions:**
- NTA officials questioned by parliamentary panel
- Investigation into insider involvement (P V Kulkarni described as main accused with NTA process links)

### Ministry of Education/Union Government Actions

**Official Statement by Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan:**
- Admitted "breach in the command chain" in NTA operations
- Announced plans to **migrate NEET to Computer-Based Testing (CBT) format from 2027** onwards
- Emphasized need for structural reforms in examination management

**Structural Changes:**
- Centre appointed **4 new officials** to NTA mid-investigation:
  - **Anuja Bapat** - Appointed as Joint Secretary, NTA (5-year term)
  - **Ruchita Vij** - Appointed as Joint Secretary, NTA (5-year term)
  - 2 Joint Directors appointed to strengthen institutional oversight

**Reform Plans:**
- Complete overhaul of National Testing Agency planned
- Upgrade of entire NTA system security and operations
- Preparation for dismissal of "long-time serving officers and employees" due to "serious negligence and failure to adhere to established security procedures"

## Leaked Paper Details

### Examination Overview
- **Exam Name**: NEET-UG 2026 (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test - Undergraduate)
- **Exam Date**: May 3, 2026
- **Total Aspirants**: 2.27 million registered
- **Test Centers**: 551 Indian cities and 14 overseas centers
- **Cancellation Date**: May 12, 2026

### Leak Characteristics
- **Type**: Large-scale coordinated paper leak with **140+ matching questions** between "guess paper" and actual exam
- **Subjects Affected**: Chemistry and Biology particularly compromised
- **Distribution Method**: WhatsApp and other digital channels through coaching centers
- **Geographic Concentration**: Sikar (Rajasthan) and surrounding areas initial hotspot
- **Money Trail**: Rs 10 lakh paid for acquisition; Rs 12 lakh charged for distribution to candidates

### Evidence of Overlap
- Chemistry teacher from Sikar compared leaked materials with official exam paper
- Found approximately **140 questions matched between guess paper and real exam**
- Questions appeared across multiple subjects, indicating systematic leak rather than coincidence

### Source of Leak
- **Primary Source**: Question paper translation process
- **Access Point**: Subject experts and teachers with legitimate access to exam material (P V Kulkarni, Manisha Havaldar, others)
- **Secondary Sources**: NTA processes and internal operations
- **Distribution**: Through network of coaching institutes and middlemen

- **Re-exam Date**: June 21, 2026
