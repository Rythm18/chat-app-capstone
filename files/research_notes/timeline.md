# NEET 2026 Exam Paper Leak: Chronological Timeline

## Timeline of Key Events

### April 2026
- **April 23, 2026**: Alleged date when chemistry questions for the NEET examination were received by Shivaraj Motegaonkar (owner of Renukai Chemistry Classes in Latur, Maharashtra), marking the beginning of the leak pipeline.

### May 2026

#### Before the Exam
- **May 3, 2026**: NEET UG 2026 examination held nationally for over 2.27 million aspirants seeking admission to undergraduate medical and dental courses across India and abroad. Exam conducted in offline pen-and-paper mode from 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM (IST).

#### Leak Discovery Period
- **Early May 2026** (Days after exam): Allegations emerged that a "guess paper" or question bank had been circulated through WhatsApp and other channels in coaching centers, particularly in Sikar, Rajasthan. The unauthorized paper contained approximately 120-140 questions that closely matched actual NEET UG 2026 exam questions, particularly in Chemistry and Biology sections. The guess paper reportedly contained ~410 total questions with ~120 matching the actual exam paper.

- **May 8, 2026**: National Testing Agency escalates the matter to concerned central agencies following initial investigations revealing overlaps between pre-circulated guess paper and actual question paper.

- **May 12, 2026**: National Testing Agency announces official cancellation of NEET UG 2026 examination in a formal press release after inputs from central agencies and law enforcement agencies confirmed significant irregularities in the examination process. NTA clarifies that:
  - Fees already paid by candidates (over 22 lakh students affected) would be fully refunded
  - No fresh registration or additional fees required
  - Existing candidate data and examination center selections would be retained
  - Government orders CBI investigation into the matter

#### Investigation Begins
- **May 15, 2026**: CBI formally takes over investigation from Rajasthan Police Special Operations Group. NTA announces re-examination would be held but specific date not yet finalized.

- **May 18, 2026**: CBI arrests Shivaraj Motegaonkar, owner of Renukai Chemistry Classes Coaching Institute in Maharashtra's Latur district, in connection with the paper leak case. CBI tells New Delhi court that Motegaonkar had received chemistry questions on April 23, 2026. Investigation reveals that alleged leaked paper was handwritten, scanned, converted into PDF files, and circulated among students at coaching centers in Rajasthan's Sikar district for Rs 2 lakh to Rs 5 lakh rupees for access.

- **May 21, 2026**: Parliamentary Standing Committee on Education summons NTA Chairperson and Higher Education Secretary to appear before the committee regarding the NEET UG 2026 paper leak scandal. During the session, several Members of Parliament (MPs) question NTA officials on steps taken to prevent future paper leaks and strengthen the examination system.

- **May 22, 2026**: NTA Chief appears before Parliament Committee and states that "NEET paper not leaked, only some questions came out," responding to the controversy and attempts to defend the integrity of the examination system.

- **May 25, 2026**: Supreme Court of India seeks status report on implementation of recommendations made by the committee constituted after its 2024 ruling on the NEET-UG paper leak issue that year. Supreme Court monitors compliance with previous recommendations designed to prevent such incidents.

- **May 26, 2026**: CBI arrests the student who obtained physics questions from Manisha Sanjay Havaldar, furthering the investigation into the leak network.

- **Late May 2026**: CBI announces arrest of several key accused including:
  - Manisha Sanjay Havaldar, headmistress of Pune-based school (arrested May 22), arrested for allegedly leaking the physics question paper; had access through appointment as subject engineer by NTA
  - Pune-based chemistry professor P V Kulkarni (described as main accused with links to NTA processes)
  - Biology professor Manisha Gurunath Mandhare
  - Additional suspects: Dr Manoj Shirure, Tejas Harshad Kumar Shah, and others remanded to judicial custody

### June 2026

#### Re-Exam Date Announcement
- **June 15, 2026** (or earlier): NTA officially announces and confirms Re-NEET 2026 exam date as June 21, 2026 (Sunday) with government approval. Official announcement includes:
  - Exam date: June 21, 2026 (Sunday)
  - Exam time: 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM (IST)
  - Mode: Offline pen-and-paper mode
  - Location: Exam centers across India and abroad
  - Fresh admit cards to be released by June 14, 2026
  - No re-registration required; no additional fees

#### Student Protests Intensify
- **June 1, 2026**: Large-scale student protests erupt outside the Ministry of Education in New Delhi. Demonstrations voice outrage over:
  - NEET-UG 2026 paper leak
  - CUET-UG exam delays
  - CBSE paper-checking portal issues
  - Students and opposition parties intensify criticism of Centre's handling of examinations

- **June 12, 2026**: Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke joins student protest in Lucknow where participants voice concerns about NEET paper leak case and other exam-related issues.

#### Ongoing Investigations & Developments
- **Early-Mid June 2026**: CBI investigation reveals that the leak originated from an organized interstate network spanning:
  - Rajasthan
  - Haryana
  - Maharashtra
  - Uttarakhand
  - Kerala
  
- **June 2026**: Indian Youth Congress members stage protest in Pune, Maharashtra, calling for action against Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. Opposition parties demand:
  - Fair and transparent investigation into the alleged leak
  - Pradhan's immediate resignation
  - Dissolution of the National Testing Agency
  - Accountability for repeated examination failures

#### Government Announcements on Future Measures
- **June 2026**: Education Ministry confirms future preventive measures:
  - NEET UG 2027 will be in Computer-Based Test (CBT) format
  - AI-based monitoring systems to be implemented
  - Biometric verification of personnel involved in exam processes
  - Encrypted question distribution protocols

## Key Figures & Numbers

### Exam Details
- **Total Aspirants Affected**: Over 2.27 million students took the May 3, 2026 exam
- **Refund Cases**: Over 22 lakh (2.2 million) candidates eligible for fee refunds
- **Questions in Leaked Guess Paper**: Approximately 410 questions total
- **Matching Questions**: 120-140 questions matched actual NEET paper
- **Money Involved in Leak**: Rs 2 lakh to Rs 5 lakh per access to leaked materials

### Investigation Status
- **Total Arrests Made**: Multiple arrests including at least 7-8 identified individuals
- **Scope of Criminal Network**: Interstate operation spanning 5 states (Rajasthan, Haryana, Maharashtra, Uttarakhand, Kerala)
- **CBI Investigation**: Ongoing as of June 2026

### Timeline Summary
- **Days from Exam to Leak Discovery**: 5-10 days
- **Days from Leak to Cancellation**: 9 days (May 3 to May 12)
- **Days from Cancellation to Re-exam**: 40 days (May 12 to June 21)
- **Total Delay from Original Exam Date**: 49 days
